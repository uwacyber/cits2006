# Project: Owner Authorisation Form

Use this form before your group does **anything active** with a community organisation's systems. "Active" means scanning, logging in, changing settings, or installing or configuring anything. Until this form is signed and uploaded to LMS, you may only do passive work: public information, site visits and conversations.

Print or copy this form, complete it with the owner, and upload a scan to LMS (Project → Owner authorisation) **before** the first active step. If the scope changes, complete a new form.

### Out of scope (always)

- Any system not listed above, including systems run by the organisation's providers (website host, cloud services, ISP) unless the provider has also agreed in writing.
- Copying, keeping or reporting personal information about the organisation's customers, members or staff.
- Denial-of-service testing, password-guessing against live accounts, phishing staff without separate written agreement, and physical intrusion.

### How findings are handled

- Findings are shared only with the owner and the CITS2006 teaching staff.
- Reports and the highlight video **do not name the organisation** and do not show identifying details. The video is shared as an unlisted link.
- The owner can ask the group to **stop** at any time, and the group stops immediately.
- Students are learners, not professional consultants, and the work is provided without warranty.
