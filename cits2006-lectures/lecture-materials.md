# Schedule and Lecture Materials

CITS2006 is taught **flipped**:

1. **Before the week:** watch that week's online lecture (up to three videos of about 15 minutes each). Videos are on LMS (Echo360), and slides are linked below. Both are released on the Friday before the week.
2. **Lab (Tuesday to Thursday):** work through the week's lab sheet with a facilitator.
3. **Workshop (Friday, whole class):** a demo, then a 65-minute CTF round on FlagBoard. Bring a charged laptop set up in Lab 0.

All deadlines are 11:59 pm AWST unless stated otherwise. If a Friday deadline falls on a public holiday, it is due the Thursday before; if a Monday deadline falls on a public holiday, it is due the Tuesday after.
